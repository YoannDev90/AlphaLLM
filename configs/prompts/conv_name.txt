Your task is to generate a concise title for a conversation.

Rules:
- The title must contain **between 2 and 5 words**.
- Use **the same language as the user's message**.
- The title should **accurately summarize the main topic** of the conversation without adding extra information.
- Avoid unnecessary words or articles; prefer **informative and meaningful terms**.
- Do not include punctuation marks or quotation marks at the end.
- **Output only:** the final title, with no explanations or additional text.

Example:
User: "Explain how photosynthesis works."
Output: "Photosynthesis Process"
